# Story-Based Content Development Framework

Use this framework to discover, document, develop, verify, adapt, approve, and reuse stories without sacrificing truth, dignity, privacy, brand voice, or human judgment.

This framework is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> A story should carry truth forward, not decorate a message with details that never happened.

---

## Framework Purpose

The Story-Based Content Development Framework helps organizations:

- Recognize stories inside ordinary experience
- Capture founder and customer knowledge
- Separate fact from memory and interpretation
- Preserve the dignity of people in the story
- Document consent and permissions
- Structure stories with tension and meaning
- Connect stories naturally to business ideas
- Use AI without inventing details
- Adapt one story across several formats
- Maintain brand voice
- Review facts, claims, and privacy
- Archive stories for responsible reuse
- Track versions, approvals, and restrictions

The framework may be used for:

- Articles
- LinkedIn posts
- Social media
- Email
- Newsletters
- Presentations
- Webinars
- Videos
- Podcasts
- Lead magnets
- Case studies
- Customer stories
- Founder stories
- Training
- Workshops
- Sales communication
- Community discussions
- Brand history
- Internal knowledge capture
- AI-assisted content development

# Core Principle

## Experience Becomes Meaning Through Reflection

An event is not automatically a story.

A story begins when someone can identify:

- What happened
- Why it mattered
- What changed
- What was learned
- What remains unresolved
- Why another person should care

The framework follows this sequence:

```text
Experience
   ↓
Memory
   ↓
Verification
   ↓
Interpretation
   ↓
Story Structure
   ↓
Business Connection
   ↓
Human Review
   ↓
Approved Content
   ↓
Responsible Reuse
```

## 38. Dialogue Rules

AI must not:

- Invent quotations
- Make people sound more eloquent
- Add jokes that were never made
- Create conflict that did not happen
- Assign motives through dialogue
- Turn a summary into a direct quotation
- Combine several conversations without disclosure

Use paraphrase when exact wording is uncertain.

Example:

```text
He explained that the project could not continue without approval.
```

instead of:

```text
“This project is dead unless you approve it today,” he said.
```

- “A powerful reminder”
- “Little did I know”
- “Everything changed in that moment”
- “This taught me an invaluable lesson”
- “In conclusion”

Replace them with:

- Specific details
- Natural reflection
- Original language
- Concrete decisions
- Verified emotion
- Real consequences
- Practical meaning

# Part Fourteen: Story Integrity Review

## 58. Fact Review

Confirm:

- [ ] Names are correct.
- [ ] Dates are correct.
- [ ] Locations are correct.
- [ ] Roles are correct.
- [ ] Sequence is correct.
- [ ] Dialogue is exact or disclosed.
- [ ] Results are accurate.
- [ ] Sources support material details.
- [ ] Uncertain details are labeled.
- [ ] AI-generated details were independently checked.

---

## 59. Meaning Review

Confirm:

- [ ] The lesson comes from the story.
- [ ] The business connection is natural.
- [ ] The story does not claim more than it proves.
- [ ] The storyteller’s interpretation remains distinct from fact.
- [ ] Another person’s motives are not invented.
- [ ] The conclusion preserves complexity.
- [ ] The story provides genuine value.

---

## 60. Permission Review

Confirm:

- [ ] Story permission is documented.
- [ ] Name permission is documented.
- [ ] Quote permission is documented.
- [ ] Image permission is documented.
- [ ] Recording permission is documented.
- [ ] Channel permission is documented.
- [ ] Repurposing permission is documented.
- [ ] Withdrawal terms are understood.
- [ ] Restrictions are visible to future users.

---

## 61. Privacy Review

Confirm:

- [ ] Personal information is minimized.
- [ ] Confidential information is removed.
- [ ] Customer data is protected.
- [ ] Employee information is protected.
- [ ] Medical details are protected.
- [ ] Financial details are protected.
- [ ] Legal details are protected.
- [ ] Location details are appropriate.
- [ ] File metadata is reviewed.
- [ ] Screenshots are reviewed.

---

## 62. Ethical Review

Confirm:

- [ ] The story does not exploit pain.
- [ ] The story does not humiliate.
- [ ] The story does not manipulate.
- [ ] The story does not rely on false urgency.
- [ ] The story respects vulnerable people.
- [ ] The audience retains meaningful choice.
- [ ] The story owner accepts responsibility for publication.
- [ ] Harm is not justified merely by possible engagement.

## Important Principle

A story is not valuable because it is dramatic.

It is valuable because it helps another person recognize something true.

AI may help organize the words.

The human must protect the people, preserve the truth, and decide what the story means.
