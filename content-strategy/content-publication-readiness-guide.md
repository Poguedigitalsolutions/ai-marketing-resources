# Content Publication Readiness Guide

Use this guide to verify that a content asset is accurate, approved, accessible, properly formatted, measurable, recoverable, and ready to publish on the intended platform.

This guide is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> Publication readiness is the final checkpoint between “this looks finished” and “this is safe, accurate, approved, and ready to represent the brand in public.”

---

## Guide Purpose

The Content Publication Readiness Guide helps organizations:

- Confirm final approval
- Select the correct file and version
- Confirm headline, hook, body, and CTA alignment
- Verify facts
- Verify claims
- Verify sources
- Verify citations
- Verify links
- Protect privacy
- Confirm permissions
- Review copyright
- Protect confidential information
- Review accessibility
- Review mobile presentation
- Match platform requirements
- Confirm metadata
- Confirm visual assets
- Confirm character limits
- Configure tracking
- Configure analytics
- Configure campaign tags
- Verify conversion destinations
- Confirm scheduling
- Assign publishing ownership
- Prepare rollback plans
- Prepare correction plans
- Prepare escalation plans
- Require human approval before publication
- Monitor post-publication performance
- Correct errors
- Archive published versions
- Retire outdated assets

This guide may be used for:

- Articles
- Blog posts
- LinkedIn articles
- Social-media posts
- Newsletters
- Emails
- Videos
- Podcasts
- Webinars
- Workshops
- Lead magnets
- Landing pages
- Case studies
- Reports
- Presentations
- Customer education
- Sales-enablement content
- Content series
- AI-assisted publishing systems

# Publication Readiness Overview

```text
Confirm the approved final asset
        ↓
Confirm the correct version
        ↓
Review headline, hook, body, and CTA alignment
        ↓
Verify facts, claims, sources, citations, and links
        ↓
Review privacy, permissions, copyright, and confidentiality
        ↓
Review accessibility and mobile presentation
        ↓
Review platform formatting and metadata
        ↓
Confirm tracking and conversion destinations
        ↓
Confirm schedule, ownership, rollback, and escalation
        ↓
Human approval
        ↓
Publish
        ↓
Monitor, correct, archive, refresh, or retire
```

## Important Principle

The publish button is small.

The responsibility attached to it is not.

A polished asset may still contain the wrong version, a broken link, an unsupported claim, missing permission, inaccessible media, private information, or a CTA that leads into the digital shrubbery.

Publication readiness is where those loose wires are found before the audience trips over them.

AI may help compare versions, identify omissions, check length, prepare metadata, and support monitoring.

A human must still decide whether the exact final asset is accurate, approved, accessible, safe, measurable, recoverable, and ready to carry the brand into public view.
