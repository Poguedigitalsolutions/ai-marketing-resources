# Content Strategy Development Guide

Use this guide to design, document, approve, execute, measure, refresh, and retire a content strategy that connects business objectives, audience needs, customer evidence, brand voice, distribution, and human judgment.

This guide is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> Content strategy is not a publishing calendar wearing a business suit. It is the system that decides what should be communicated, to whom, for what purpose, through which channel, with which evidence, and under whose approval.

---

## Guide Purpose

The Content Strategy Development Guide helps organizations:

- Define business objectives
- Identify audience needs
- Map content to customer journey stages
- Establish content pillars
- Develop themes
- Select formats and channels
- Prioritize publishing
- Use customer evidence
- Capture founder and subject-matter expertise
- Preserve stories and original insight
- Establish source requirements
- Align content with positioning
- Maintain brand voice
- Develop message hierarchies
- Define calls to action
- Identify content gaps
- Review competitive context
- Plan repurposing
- Plan distribution
- Assign ownership
- Establish editorial governance
- Require human approval
- Protect privacy
- Respect permissions and copyright
- Improve accessibility
- Use AI responsibly
- Measure performance
- Record learning
- Refresh outdated assets
- Retire content responsibly

This guide may be used for:

- Company-wide content strategy
- Founder-led brands
- Thought leadership
- Educational content
- Social-media strategy
- Email strategy
- Blog strategy
- Video strategy
- Podcast strategy
- Newsletter strategy
- Community strategy
- Lead-generation content
- Sales-enablement content
- Customer-onboarding content
- Customer-retention content
- Internal communications
- Campaign content
- AI-assisted content systems

```text
Define business objectives
        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
Review privacy, accessibility, sources, and risk
        ↓

        ↓
Publish and distribute
        ↓
Measure, learn, refresh, or retire
```

## Important Principle

A strong content strategy does not begin with the question, “What should we post this week?”

It begins with better questions:

- Who are we trying to help?
- What are they trying to understand?
- Where are they in the journey?
- What evidence do we have?
- What can we contribute that is genuinely useful?
- What should happen next?
- What requires human judgment?

AI may help research, organize, outline, draft, adapt, distribute, and analyze.

A human must still decide what deserves to be said, what may be shared, what the evidence supports, and whether the final content strengthens trust rather than merely increasing volume.
