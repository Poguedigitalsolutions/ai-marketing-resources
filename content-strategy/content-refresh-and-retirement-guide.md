# Content Refresh and Retirement Guide

Use this guide to decide whether a published content asset should remain active, receive a minor update, undergo a major rewrite, be consolidated, redirected, archived, or retired.

This guide is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> Content should not remain active merely because nobody remembered to remove it. Every public asset should still be accurate, relevant, useful, accessible, permitted, and worthy of representing the brand.

---

## Guide Purpose

The Content Refresh and Retirement Guide helps organizations:

- Identify content that requires review
- Decide whether content should remain active
- Distinguish minor updates from major rewrites
- Refresh outdated facts
- Refresh outdated sources
- Refresh broken links
- Refresh outdated examples
- Refresh outdated offers
- Refresh outdated calls to action
- Review audience relevance
- Review customer journey alignment
- Review brand positioning
- Review performance
- Review permissions
- Review copyright status
- Review privacy
- Review accessibility
- Review platform requirements
- Consolidate overlapping assets
- Redirect outdated URLs
- Archive historical content
- Retire content responsibly
- Preserve source history
- Preserve approval records
- Preserve analytics
- Preserve version history
- Update derivative assets
- Update repurposed assets
- Update automated campaigns
- Remove outdated content from AI retrieval systems
- Communicate major corrections
- Communicate major retirements
- Assign ownership

- Use AI responsibly

# Refresh and Retirement Overview

```text
Identify the asset
        ↓
Confirm the exact published version
        ↓
Review accuracy, relevance, audience fit, performance, permissions, links, and accessibility
        ↓
Classify the issue
        ↓
Choose keep, minor refresh, major rewrite, consolidate, redirect, archive, or retire
        ↓
Assign ownership and approvals
        ↓
Update related and derivative assets
        ↓
Update AI retrieval systems and automations
        ↓
Publish the approved change
        ↓
Monitor, document, and schedule the next review
```

# Phase Fifteen: Classify the Required Change

## 35. Keep Active

Use when:

- The content remains accurate
- The audience remains relevant
- The offer remains current
- Permissions remain valid
- Accessibility remains acceptable
- Performance remains useful
- No meaningful update is required

---

## 36. Minor Refresh

Use when changes are limited to:

- Broken links
- Small factual updates
- New statistics
- Small wording changes
- CTA updates
- Metadata updates
- Image replacement
- Accessibility improvements
- Minor formatting changes

A minor refresh should not materially change the strategy, angle, or promise.

---

## 37. Major Rewrite

Use when:

- The audience changed
- The topic changed materially
- The angle changed
- The structure no longer works
- Evidence changed materially
- Positioning changed
- Offer changed materially
- The asset contains substantial outdated content
- Brand voice changed
- Accessibility requires significant reconstruction

---

## 38. Consolidate

Use when:

- Several assets overlap
- Multiple assets compete for the same audience question
- Each asset is too thin
- Search navigation would improve
- A stronger comprehensive resource can replace them
- Maintenance can be reduced

---

## 39. Redirect

Use when:

- A stronger replacement exists
- The URL has meaningful history
- Search or referral traffic should be preserved
- Internal links should point to a new resource
- A campaign page was replaced

---

## 40. Archive

Use when:

- The asset has historical value
- The content should not remain actively promoted
- The asset should remain available for records
- A campaign ended
- A report reflects a past period
- Approval and analytics history should remain accessible

---

## 41. Retire

Use when:

- Information is materially outdated
- Evidence no longer supports the asset
- Audience need no longer exists
- Offer no longer exists
- Permission was withdrawn
- Copyright status changed
- Content creates confusion
- Content creates risk
- A stronger replacement exists
- Maintenance exceeds value
- The content cannot be repaired responsibly

---

## 69. Prohibited AI Uses

AI should not:

- Make the final retirement decision
- Invent outdated claims
- Invent permissions
- Invent ownership
- Remove content automatically without approval
- Rewrite material claims without review
- Delete historical records
- Decide legal or compliance risk
- Replace subject-matter judgment
- Hide caveats
- Publish changes without approval

## Important Principle

A content library is a living system, not a digital attic.

Every asset that remains active continues speaking for the brand.

Some need a fresh coat of evidence.

Some need a new structure.

Some should merge with stronger resources.

Some should be placed in the archive with their history intact.

Some should be retired before they wander back into an AI system wearing an outdated statistic and pretending nothing happened.

AI may help identify aging assets, broken links, overlap, outdated language, and possible retrieval risks.

A human must still decide what remains true, what still serves the audience, what deserves repair, what should be preserved, and what needs a respectful exit.
