# Quick Start Guide

Welcome to the **AI Marketing Resources** library.

This repository contains practical prompts, templates, checklists, frameworks, workflows, customer-research tools, content-strategy guides, and responsible AI resources.

You do not need to use everything at once.

Start with the problem you are trying to solve, follow the recommended path, and keep human judgment in the loop.

> AI should amplify human expression, not replace it.

## The Recommended Five-Step Method

For most projects, use this sequence:

### 1. Define the context

Clarify:

- The business objective
- The intended audience
- The customer journey stage
- The offer or message
- The approved source material
- The brand voice
- The risks and constraints
- The required human reviewers

Start with the [Context Before Creation Framework](frameworks/context-before-creation.md).

### 2. Prepare the inputs

Gather:

- Customer evidence
- Brand examples
- Source documents
- Facts and claims
- Stories
- Channel requirements
- Privacy and confidentiality boundaries

Use the [Prompt Preparation Checklist](checklists/prompt-preparation-checklist.md).

### 3. Use AI for structured assistance

AI can help with:

- Research organization
- Comparison
- Summarization
- Outlining
- Drafting
- Repurposing
- Workflow documentation
- Pattern identification

AI should not invent evidence, fabricate quotations, expose confidential information, or make unreviewed high-risk decisions.

### 4. Conduct human review

Review for:

- Accuracy
- Source integrity
- Brand voice
- Privacy
- Consent
- Accessibility
- Ethical communication
- Platform requirements
- Legal or compliance risk
- Final approval

Use the [AI-Generated Content Review Checklist](checklists/ai-generated-content-review-checklist.md).

### 5. Publish, monitor, and improve

After publication or execution:

- Verify the live version
- Monitor responses and performance
- Correct errors
- Record lessons
- Refresh outdated resources
- Retire ineffective or unsafe content

Use the [Content Performance Review Guide](content-strategy/content-performance-review-guide.md) and [Content Refresh and Retirement Guide](content-strategy/content-refresh-and-retirement-guide.md).

## Before You Use Any Prompt

Complete these five checks:

- Do I know what outcome I want?
- Have I provided enough business and audience context?
- Have I separated facts from assumptions?
- Have I removed confidential or unnecessary personal information?
- Do I know who must review and approve the result?

For a deeper review, use the [Prompt Preparation Checklist](checklists/prompt-preparation-checklist.md).

---

## Before You Publish or Send Anything

Confirm that:

- The content is accurate
- Important claims are supported
- Quotations are genuine and approved
- The message sounds like the brand
- Confidential information is protected
- Permissions and consent are documented
- Accessibility has been considered
- The final version has human approval
- Someone is responsible for monitoring and correction

Use the [AI-Generated Content Review Checklist](checklists/ai-generated-content-review-checklist.md).

## Core Principle

AI can prepare the work.

Humans remain responsible for the message, the decision, and the consequences.
