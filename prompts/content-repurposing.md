# Content Repurposing Prompt

Use this prompt to turn one article, video, transcript, workshop, story, newsletter, or presentation into a coordinated content ecosystem without creating a parade of near-identical posts.

This resource is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> Repurposing should extend an idea, not photocopy it.

---

## Purpose

This prompt helps transform one strong source into multiple useful assets while preserving:

- The central idea
- Brand voice
- Factual accuracy
- Audience relevance
- Platform context
- Personal stories
- Human judgment
- Meaningful variation

The goal is not to squeeze every sentence into every platform.

The goal is to discover the different conversations, lessons, questions, examples, and resources already living inside the source.

## Before You Begin

Gather the original source and any context that should guide the work.

Useful inputs include:

- Original content
- Brand voice profile
- Customer avatar
- Intended audience
- Main offer
- Existing calls to action
- Supporting evidence
- Personal stories
- Customer questions
- Platform goals
- Publishing schedule
- Links
- Visual assets
- Previous related content

Do not ask AI to invent facts, stories, customer results, quotations, statistics, examples, or personal experiences.

---

## Master Prompt

Copy the prompt below into your preferred AI assistant.

Replace the bracketed sections with your information.

```text
You are helping me repurpose one source into a coordinated content ecosystem.

Your job is to preserve the central meaning while adapting each asset for its platform, audience, format, and purpose.

Do not produce multiple copies of the same post with minor wording changes.

Do not invent facts, statistics, quotations, customer stories, personal experiences, research findings, credentials, or results.

Separate confirmed information from interpretation.

When the source does not support a claim, say so.

CREATOR OR BRAND:

[Enter the person, company, or brand.]

BRAND VOICE:

[Paste the brand voice summary, examples, voice rules, signature language, voice dos, and voice don’ts.]

SOURCE CONTENT:

[Paste the complete article, transcript, story, presentation, notes, or other source.]

SOURCE TYPE:

[Article, video, transcript, workshop, story, newsletter, podcast, case study, or another format.]

CORE IDEA:

[State the central idea in one or two sentences.]

TARGET AUDIENCE:

[Describe the intended audience.]

AUDIENCE PROBLEM OR INTEREST:

[Describe the problem, question, tension, desire, or opportunity that makes the source relevant.]

PRIMARY CONTENT GOAL:

[Awareness, education, engagement, authority, lead generation, community growth, offer promotion, or another goal.]

SECONDARY CONTENT GOAL:

[Describe any supporting goal.]

DESIRED AUDIENCE RESPONSE:

[What should the audience think, feel, discuss, or do?]

PLATFORMS:

[List the platforms you want content created for.]

PUBLISHING PERIOD:

[One week, two weeks, thirty days, campaign period, or another timeframe.]

PRIMARY CALL TO ACTION:

[Describe the main next step.]

SECONDARY CALL TO ACTION:

[Describe a lower-commitment next step.]

LINKS OR DESTINATIONS:

[Add relevant article, community, service, event, resource, or website links.]

IMPORTANT FACTS:

[List facts that may be treated as confirmed.]

CLAIMS REQUIRING VERIFICATION:

[List dates, statistics, quotations, research claims, or assumptions that must be checked.]

STORIES OR EXAMPLES THAT MAY BE USED:

[Describe true stories or examples.]

WORDS, CLAIMS, OR STYLES TO AVOID:

[List language that feels generic, exaggerated, repetitive, promotional, corporate, false, or inconsistent with the brand.]

Using only the supplied information, create a Content Repurposing Report with the following sections:

1. Source Analysis

Identify:

- Central idea
- Primary argument
- Supporting ideas
- Personal stories
- Useful examples
- Questions raised
- Practical lessons
- Memorable language
- Strong quotations from the supplied source
- Claims requiring verification
- Information that should not be reused without context

2. Content Pillars

Identify five to seven content pillars contained within the source.

For each pillar, include:

- Pillar name
- Main idea
- Audience relevance
- Best platform
- Best format
- Possible CTA
- Risk of repetition

3. Content Atomization Map

Break the source into reusable content units such as:

- Claims
- Lessons
- Questions
- Stories
- Examples
- Frameworks
- Definitions
- Warnings
- Contrasts
- Objections
- Quotable lines
- Action steps
- Visual concepts
- Discussion prompts

Do not remove important context merely to create a shorter asset.

4. Angle Library

Create fifteen distinct content angles.

For each angle, include:

- Angle title
- Central point
- Intended audience reaction
- Best platform
- Best format
- Relationship to the original source
- What makes it different from the other angles

5. Hook Library

Create twenty hooks across these styles:

- Direct
- Question-based
- Story-first
- Problem-first
- Contrarian
- Lesson-first
- Framework-first
- Curiosity-based
- Practical
- Reflective
- Customer-language
- Short declaration

Avoid clickbait and unsupported promises.

6. LinkedIn Assets

Create:

- Three thought-leadership posts
- Two personal-story posts
- Two educational posts
- One contrarian post
- One question post
- One article teaser
- One carousel outline

Each asset should have a different purpose and opening.

7. X Assets

Create:

- Ten standalone posts
- Two five-post threads
- Three question posts
- Three concise quote-style posts
- One poll concept

Each post should be understandable without depending on hidden context.

8. Facebook Assets

Create:

- Two conversational posts
- Two story posts
- One community question
- One article promotion post
- One behind-the-scenes post

The tone should feel natural and less formal than LinkedIn.

9. Instagram Assets

Create:

- Three short captions
- Two story-based captions
- One carousel outline
- One Reel script
- Five Story prompts
- Three quote graphic concepts

10. TikTok and Short-Video Assets

Create five scripts lasting 30 to 60 seconds.

For each script, include:

- Hook
- Spoken body
- Visual direction
- On-screen text
- Closing line
- CTA

The scripts should sound natural when spoken aloud.

11. YouTube Assets

Create:

- One long-form video outline
- Three Shorts concepts
- Two Community posts
- One title and thumbnail concept
- One description
- One pinned-comment CTA

12. Email Assets

Create:

- One newsletter introduction
- Three short nurture emails
- One article-promotion email
- One plain-text email
- Five subject lines
- Five preview-text options

13. Skool Community Assets

Create:

- One teaching post
- One discussion prompt
- One action challenge
- One workshop prompt
- One resource-sharing post
- One accountability check-in

14. Article and Newsletter Extensions

Create:

- Three follow-up article ideas
- One deeper research article
- One beginner-friendly article
- One opinion piece
- One case-study concept
- One FAQ article
- One newsletter series outline

15. Lead Magnet Opportunities

Identify five possible lead magnets that could grow naturally from the source.

For each, include:

- Title
- Format
- Problem solved
- Quick win
- Relationship to the source
- CTA
- Development effort

16. Visual Content Opportunities

Create concepts for:

- Quote graphics
- Infographics
- Carousels
- Diagrams
- Checklists
- Timelines
- Comparison charts
- Framework graphics
- Hero banners
- Short-video visuals

Do not invent statistics or visual data.

17. Story Extraction

Identify every true story in the source.

For each story, explain:

- What happened
- Why it matters
- Main lesson
- Best platform
- Best format
- Context that must remain
- Risk of oversimplification

18. Quote Extraction

Extract concise, meaningful quotations from the supplied source.

For each quotation, include:

- Exact wording
- Context
- Suggested use
- Platform
- Visual concept
- Whether editing would change the meaning

Do not manufacture quotations.

19. Question Bank

Create twenty audience questions derived from the source.

Organize them into:

- Reflection questions
- Experience questions
- Opinion questions
- Practical questions
- Objection questions
- Decision questions
- Community discussion questions

20. Objection Content

Identify objections or skeptical questions raised by the source.

Create content that addresses each objection through:

- Short post
- FAQ answer
- Email
- Video concept
- Article section
- Community discussion

21. Platform Adaptation Audit

For every generated asset, evaluate:

- Platform fit
- Length
- Opening
- Tone
- CTA
- Formatting
- Audience expectation
- Whether it feels copied from another platform

Rewrite anything that feels transplanted rather than adapted.

22. Repetition Audit

Identify repeated:

- Hooks
- Arguments
- Stories
- Examples
- Phrases
- CTAs
- Structures
- Conclusions

Rewrite or remove repetitive assets.

23. Brand Voice Review

Review all assets for:

- Tone
- Vocabulary
- Rhythm
- Sentence length
- Storytelling
- Warmth
- Authority
- Signature language
- Voice dos
- Voice don’ts
- Generic AI phrasing
- Excessive polish
- Overpromotion
- Emotional authenticity

24. Generic Language Audit

Highlight language that sounds:

- Corporate
- Artificial
- Predictable
- Inflated
- Promotional
- Emotionally empty
- Detached from the creator
- Detached from the audience
- Repetitive
- Overly polished

Rewrite those sections naturally.

25. Factuality Review

Create a table containing:

- Claim
- Original source location
- Verification status
- Context required
- Risk level
- Recommended action

Do not create sources that were not supplied.

26. Context Preservation Review

Identify content units that may become misleading when shortened.

Explain:

- What context is missing
- Why the shorter version may distort the message
- What minimum context should remain
- Whether the asset should be removed

27. CTA Map

Create calls to action for different levels of readiness:

- Comment
- Reply
- Share an experience
- Read the full article
- Download a resource
- Join a community
- Watch a video
- Subscribe
- Book a conversation
- Explore a service

Match each CTA to the appropriate asset.

28. Publishing Sequence

Create a fourteen-day publishing calendar.

For each day, include:

- Platform
- Content asset
- Content pillar
- Format
- Goal
- CTA
- Relationship to previous content
- Reason for the sequence position

Avoid publishing the same idea repeatedly on consecutive days.

29. Thirty-Day Expansion Plan

Create a thirty-day content ecosystem containing:

- Weekly themes
- Anchor content
- Supporting content
- Community discussions
- Email
- Video
- Article
- Lead magnet
- Promotional moments
- Reflection or review days

30. Content Funnel Map

Map the content across:

- Discovery
- Awareness
- Education
- Trust
- Consideration
- Decision
- Onboarding
- Advocacy

Explain which assets belong at each stage and why.

31. Content Dependency Map

Identify which assets require:

- The original article
- A visual
- Additional research
- A personal story
- Customer evidence
- Approval
- A destination link
- A lead magnet
- A video recording
- A design asset

32. Human Approval Checkpoints

Identify where human review is required before:

- Publishing factual claims
- Using personal stories
- Naming customers
- Quoting sources
- Promoting an offer
- Discussing sensitive topics
- Using regulated claims
- Scheduling automation

33. Performance Measurement Plan

Recommend metrics for each format, including:

- Impressions
- Reach
- Engagement
- Comments
- Saves
- Shares
- Clicks
- Video completion
- Replies
- Email opens
- Email clicks
- Community participation
- Downloads
- Leads
- Booked calls
- Conversions

Explain what each metric can and cannot reveal.

34. Content Refresh Plan

Explain how to update the source later using:

- New research
- New customer questions
- New examples
- New stories
- Performance data
- New objections
- Platform changes
- Seasonal context
- Business changes

35. Final Repurposing Summary

Finish with:

- Source title
- Central idea
- Primary audience
- Number of content pillars
- Strongest content angle
- Best platform
- Best lead magnet opportunity
- Primary CTA
- Main factuality risk
- Main repetition risk
- Recommended publishing period
- One-sentence ecosystem strategy

36. Human Review Questions

Finish by asking:

- Is the central idea preserved?
- Is every story true?
- Is every quotation accurate?
- Are all claims supported?
- Does each asset have a distinct purpose?
- Is the content genuinely adapted?
- Does it sound like the creator?
- Has repetition been removed?
- Has important context been preserved?
- Does every CTA fit the audience’s readiness?
- Has confidential information been excluded?
- Would the creator defend every asset publicly?
```

## Recommended Follow-Up Questions

### Follow-Up 1: Remove the Clones

```text
Compare every generated asset and identify where the hooks, arguments, stories, wording, structure, or calls to action feel duplicated. Rewrite them so each asset has a distinct purpose.
```

### Follow-Up 2: Preserve the Context

```text
Identify every short-form asset that risks oversimplifying or distorting the original source. Explain what context is missing and revise the asset.
```

### Follow-Up 3: Strengthen Platform Fit

```text
Review every asset for platform fit. Rewrite anything that feels copied from LinkedIn, email, an article, or another platform rather than naturally created for its destination.
```

### Follow-Up 4: Build the Content Calendar

```text
Create a thirty-day publishing calendar from these assets. Balance education, storytelling, engagement, authority, community, and promotion without repeating the same idea too often.
```

### Follow-Up 5: Create the Content Funnel

```text
Organize the assets into a customer journey from discovery through trust, consideration, decision, onboarding, and advocacy. Identify missing content at each stage.
```

### Follow-Up 6: Restore the Human Voice

```text
Identify language that sounds automated, generic, corporate, overly polished, or emotionally empty. Rewrite it in the supplied brand voice.
```

## Human Review Checklist

Before publishing, confirm:

- Is the original meaning intact?
- Is every story true?
- Is every quotation accurate?
- Are all claims supported?
- Are interpretations clearly labeled?
- Does each asset serve a distinct purpose?
- Is every asset adapted for its platform?
- Has repeated wording been removed?
- Has repeated structure been removed?
- Has necessary context been preserved?
- Does the content sound recognizable?
- Are CTAs proportionate?
- Are links correct?
- Has confidential information been excluded?
- Would the creator defend every asset publicly?

---

## Important Principle

A strong source is not a loaf of content to be sliced into thinner and thinner pieces.

It is a seed.

Repurposing allows that seed to grow into different forms, each suited to its own soil.
