# AI Marketing Resources Roadmap

This roadmap outlines the current development direction for the **AI Marketing Resources** repository.

The goal is to build a practical, human-centered library that helps founders, consultants, service providers, marketers, educators, and small businesses use artificial intelligence without losing accuracy, judgment, privacy, voice, or accountability.

> AI should amplify human expression, not replace it.

## Guiding Priorities

Future development should prioritize:

1. Practical usefulness
2. Human accountability
3. Accuracy and source integrity
4. Brand voice preservation
5. Customer evidence
6. Privacy and confidentiality
7. Accessibility
8. Responsible automation
9. Clear documentation
10. Continuous improvement

---

## What Will Not Be Prioritized

This repository will not prioritize:

- Content volume for its own sake
- Fully autonomous high-risk communication
- Fabricated customer insights
- Unverified factual claims
- Manipulative persuasion
- Privacy-invasive targeting
- Generic AI-generated filler
- Tools that remove necessary human judgment
- Resources created only to make the library appear larger

Quality matters more than quantity.
