# Fact and Source Verification Checklist

Use this checklist to verify factual statements, sources, quotations, statistics, research findings, customer claims, and AI-assisted content before publication, presentation, distribution, or automated use.

This resource is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> A source is not decoration. It is the bridge between a claim and the evidence supporting it.

---

## Purpose

This checklist helps confirm that:

- Facts are accurate
- Names, titles, dates, and locations are current
- Numerical claims are calculated correctly
- Sources are credible and relevant
- Primary sources are preferred
- Quotations are exact
- Statistics preserve their original context
- Research findings are represented fairly
- Conflicting evidence is acknowledged
- Uncertainty is communicated honestly
- Citations point to the correct source
- Links work
- AI-generated facts are independently verified
- Fabricated sources are detected
- Claims do not exceed their evidence
- Corrections can be made quickly
- Verification decisions receive human approval
- Time-sensitive information has a refresh date

Use it for:

- Articles
- Blog posts
- LinkedIn articles
- Social posts
- Newsletters
- Email campaigns
- Lead magnets
- Reports
- White papers
- Case studies
- Presentations
- Sales materials
- Proposals
- Web pages
- Training materials
- Videos
- Podcasts
- Webinar scripts
- Research summaries
- Customer stories
- Executive communication
- AI-generated content
- Automated communication
- Public claims
- Internal decision support

## 39. Fabricated Source Detection

Warning signs include:

- A plausible title that cannot be found
- A real author attached to a nonexistent paper
- An incorrect journal name
- A fake DOI
- A broken URL with realistic formatting
- A quotation missing from the source
- A statistic not present in the cited report
- A citation combining details from multiple sources
- A current event with no authoritative reporting
- A source that cites the AI-generated text itself

Confirm:

- [ ] Each source exists.
- [ ] Each source is authentic.
- [ ] Each source contains the cited information.
- [ ] Bibliographic details match.
- [ ] No source was accepted merely because it sounded credible.
