# Article Publishing Checklist

Use this checklist to review, approve, publish, monitor, refresh, and eventually retire an article.

This resource is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> Publishing is not the final step. It is the moment responsibility becomes public.

---

## Purpose

This checklist helps confirm that an article has:

- A clear purpose
- A defined audience
- An appropriate customer journey stage
- A useful headline
- A strong opening
- A logical structure
- Accurate facts
- Verified sources
- Supported claims
- Approved quotations
- Recognizable brand voice
- Original human perspective
- An appropriate call to action
- Accessible formatting
- Working links
- Approved images
- Privacy protection
- Required permissions
- Human approval
- Publication monitoring
- A refresh or retirement plan

Use it for:

- LinkedIn articles
- Website articles
- Blog posts
- Writing.io articles
- Substack posts
- Thought-leadership articles
- Educational articles
- Customer stories
- Industry analysis
- Founder stories
- Case-study articles
- Opinion articles
- Research summaries
- Long-form social content
- Newsletter articles
