# Customer Journey Mapping Framework

Use this framework to understand how people move from first discovering a problem to becoming informed customers, successful users, returning buyers, and advocates.

This framework is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> A customer journey is not the path a business hopes people will follow. It is the path people actually experience.

# Core Principle

## The Journey Belongs to the Customer

Businesses often organize work around internal departments:

```text
Marketing
Sales
Operations
Support
Retention
```

Customers do not experience those departments as separate systems.

They experience one continuous relationship.

A customer may move through:

```text
A question
   ↓
A search
   ↓
A useful piece of content
   ↓
A conversation
   ↓
An evaluation
   ↓
A decision
   ↓
An onboarding experience
   ↓
A result
   ↓
A renewed decision
   ↓
A recommendation
```

Every handoff affects the customer’s understanding, confidence, and trust.

# The Customer Journey Map

This framework uses ten stages:

1. Discovery
2. Awareness
3. Problem Recognition
4. Education
5. Evaluation
6. Decision
7. Onboarding
8. Adoption
9. Retention
10. Advocacy

Some businesses may combine or rename stages.

The important requirement is that each stage represents a meaningful change in the customer’s:

- Knowledge
- Belief
- confidence
- behavior
- relationship
- commitment

## 25. Bottleneck Analysis

A bottleneck is the point where progress slows, stops, or reverses.

For each bottleneck, ask:

1. What action should occur?
2. What action actually occurs?
3. What prevents movement?
4. Is the cause informational, emotional, technical, financial, or operational?
5. Which team owns the problem?
6. What evidence confirms the bottleneck?
7. What change should be tested?

## 47. Metric Caution

Metrics can indicate behavior.

They do not automatically explain motivation.

For example:

- A low click rate may indicate weak relevance, poor timing, or a technical problem.
- A high open rate does not prove trust.
- A conversion does not prove long-term value.
- A renewal does not prove satisfaction.
- A cancellation does not always indicate failure.
- Engagement does not automatically equal business impact.

Combine quantitative data with customer feedback.

# Part Fifteen: AI-Assisted Journey Mapping

## 55. Appropriate AI Uses

AI may assist with:

- Organizing interview notes
- Grouping customer questions
- Identifying possible journey stages
- Summarizing touchpoints
- Drafting journey maps
- Identifying content gaps
- Generating interview questions
- Drafting message options
- Comparing customer segments

---

## 56. AI Limitations

AI should not:

- Invent customer quotations
- Invent research findings
- Invent motivations
- Infer sensitive traits
- Treat assumptions as facts
- Replace customer interviews
- Decide high-risk actions without human review
- Publish journey-based communication automatically
- Ignore contradictory evidence
- Flatten different customer groups into one profile

---

- Human owner
- Suggested metric

Separate verified customer evidence from assumptions.

Do not invent quotations, customer stories, statistics, motivations, or outcomes.

Identify missing information and recommend focused research questions.

Provide the result for human review.
