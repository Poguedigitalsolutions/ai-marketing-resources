# AI-Generated Content Review Checklist

Use this checklist to review AI-assisted content before it is published, sent, presented, scheduled, automated, or approved for reuse.

This resource is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> AI can prepare the work. A person remains responsible for the message.

## 2. Review Outcome

Select one:

- [ ] **Pass**
- [ ] **Conditional Pass**
- [ ] **Fail**
- [ ] **Escalate for Specialized Review**

### Pass

The content fulfills its purpose, follows critical requirements, handles evidence responsibly, protects private information, preserves the brand voice, and is ready for final human approval.

### Conditional Pass

The content is usable after clearly identified corrections are completed.

### Fail

The content contains significant factual, source, privacy, consent, brand, accessibility, ethical, structural, or instruction-following problems.

### Escalate

The content requires review from an appropriate professional, such as:

- Legal
- Privacy
- Security
- Accessibility
- Compliance
- Medical
- Financial
- Human resources
- Executive leadership
- Crisis communications
- Other qualified reviewer
