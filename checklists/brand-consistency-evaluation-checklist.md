# Brand Consistency Evaluation Checklist

Use this checklist to evaluate whether a brand remains recognizable, accurate, trustworthy, and aligned across content, campaigns, customer experiences, visual assets, AI-assisted outputs, and communication channels.

This resource is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> Brand consistency is not making everything identical. It is making every expression feel connected to the same identity.

---

This checklist helps evaluate:

- Brand identity

- Audience alignment

## 3. Scoring Scale

Use the following scale:

### 5: Strong

The brand element is clear, current, recognizable, approved, and applied consistently.

### 4: Good

The element is generally consistent with minor variation that does not damage recognition or trust.

### 3: Mixed

The element is recognizable but inconsistently applied or insufficiently documented.

### 2: Weak

The element frequently conflicts with approved guidance or creates confusion.

### 1: Critical

The element is missing, inaccurate, misleading, off-brand, or creates significant risk.

### Not Applicable

The element does not apply to the asset, channel, campaign, or organization being reviewed.
