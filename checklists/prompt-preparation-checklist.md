# Prompt Preparation Checklist

Use this checklist before submitting a prompt to an AI system, adding it to a workflow, sharing it with a team, or approving it for repeated use.

This resource is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> Better prompts begin before the first instruction is written.

---

## Purpose

This checklist helps confirm that a prompt includes:

- A clear task
- A defined outcome
- Relevant audience context
- Business and customer information
- Approved source material
- Known facts
- Visible assumptions
- Missing-information rules
- Brand voice guidance
- Format requirements
- Constraints
- Privacy protections
- Examples
- Edge cases
- Testing criteria
- Human review
- Final approval

Use it for:

- Content prompts
- Research prompts
- Customer-analysis prompts
- Email prompts
- Social-media prompts
- Article prompts
- Marketing prompts
- Sales-support prompts
- Reporting prompts
- Workflow prompts
- Automation prompts
- Custom GPT instructions
- AI assistants
- Knowledge-base systems
- Internal team prompts
- Client-facing prompt libraries
- Reusable prompt templates
- Agent instructions
