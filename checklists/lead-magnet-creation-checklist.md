# Lead Magnet Creation Checklist

Use this checklist to plan, create, review, deliver, measure, refresh, and retire a lead magnet that solves a specific audience problem and leads naturally to an appropriate next step.

This resource is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> A lead magnet should earn attention by being useful, not capture contact information by disguising a sales pitch as help.

---

## Purpose

This checklist helps confirm that a lead magnet has:

- A defined audience
- A specific problem
- A practical outcome
- An appropriate format
- A clear title
- A focused scope
- Useful and actionable content
- Accurate facts and supported claims
- Recognizable brand voice
- Original human expertise
- Accessible design
- Privacy protections
- Appropriate consent
- A functioning landing page
- Reliable delivery
- A proportionate follow-up sequence
- Human approval
- Measurement
- A refresh and retirement plan

Use it for:

- Checklists
- Guides
- Workbooks
- Templates
- Assessments
- Quizzes
- Reports
- Resource lists
- Swipe files
- Calculators
- Worksheets
- Email courses
- Video training
- Mini-courses
- Prompt libraries
- Customer journey maps
- Planning tools
- Downloadable PDFs
- Interactive resources
