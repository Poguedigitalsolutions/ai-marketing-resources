# Long-Form Content Repurposing Checklist

Use this checklist to transform one approved article, transcript, video, podcast, workshop, presentation, report, or customer story into a coordinated set of channel-specific assets without producing repetitive content clones.

This resource is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> Repurposing should extend an idea’s usefulness, not photocopy it across the internet.

---

## Purpose

This checklist helps confirm that repurposed content:

- Begins with an approved source
- Preserves the original meaning
- Protects facts, claims, and quotations
- Matches the intended audience
- Fits the customer journey stage
- Adapts to each platform
- Preserves brand voice
- Uses appropriate calls to action
- Respects privacy and permissions
- Meets accessibility requirements
- Receives human review
- Is scheduled intentionally
- Is measured appropriately
- Can be corrected, refreshed, or retired

Use it for repurposing:

- Articles
- Blog posts
- LinkedIn articles
- Newsletters
- Podcast episodes
- Video transcripts
- Webinars
- Workshops
- Presentations
- Case studies
- Customer interviews
- Research reports
- Lead magnets
- Training sessions
- Founder stories
- Community discussions
- Livestreams
- Long-form social posts
- Internal knowledge

4. Correct the source.
5. Correct each derivative.
6. Record the change.
7. Notify affected audiences when appropriate.
8. Confirm links and automation.
9. Review the process failure.
