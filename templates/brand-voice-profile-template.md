# Brand Voice Profile Template

Use this template to document how a brand communicates, what it believes, how it should sound, and what makes its voice recognizable.

This resource is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> Brand voice is not decoration. It is identity expressed through language.

---

## How to Use This Template

Complete this profile using evidence from:

- Founder interviews
- Customer conversations
- Approved articles
- Social media posts
- Emails
- Videos
- Presentations
- Website copy
- Sales conversations
- Internal documents
- Brand guidelines
- Customer testimonials
- Stories
- Frequently asked questions
- Existing messaging

Do not invent voice characteristics merely because they sound attractive.

Every important conclusion should be supported by examples, source material, or direct approval from the person or organization represented.

## 18. Voice Don’ts

- Use artificial enthusiasm
- Make unsupported claims
- Create false urgency
- Use manipulative scarcity
- Invent stories
- Invent customer outcomes
- Overuse jargon
- Sound more certain than the evidence supports

- Use language the founder would never say
- Publish sensitive information

## 29. Brand Voice Review Checklist

Before publishing, confirm:

- Does this sound recognizable?
- Does it reflect the brand’s values?
- Is the audience clear?
- Is the tone appropriate?
- Is the language natural?
- Are the sentences consistent with approved examples?
- Are signature phrases used sparingly?
- Are stories accurate?
- Are claims supported?
- Are assumptions visible?
- Is uncertainty acknowledged?
- Is the CTA proportionate?
- Is the content free from unnecessary pressure?
- Is confidential information protected?
- Is the platform style appropriate?
- Has a human approved the final version?
