# Customer Interview Template

Use this template to plan, conduct, document, analyze, and review customer interviews without replacing real customer evidence with assumptions.

This resource is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> Customer interviews are not opportunities to prove an idea. They are opportunities to discover what is true.

## 3. Research Guardrails

The interview should:

- Invite honest answers
- Avoid leading the participant
- Ask about real behavior
- Ask about specific situations
- Preserve exact language
- Distinguish memory from confirmed facts
- Respect privacy
- Allow the participant to skip questions
- Avoid pressuring the participant
- Record consent
- Protect confidential information
- Separate observation from interpretation
- Avoid presenting one interview as proof of a market-wide pattern

The interview should not:

- Sell during the research portion
- Defend the business
- Correct the participant’s opinion
- Teach the participant what to say
- Suggest the desired answer
- Promise confidentiality that cannot be maintained
- Record without permission
- Invent quotations
- Combine multiple participants into a false quotation
- Present assumptions as findings

## 35. Questions to Avoid

Avoid questions such as:

- Wouldn’t you agree that [preferred answer]?
- Do you think our idea is good?
- Would you buy this?
- How much would you pay for an imaginary product?
- Don’t you think this feature would help?
- Which of these benefits do you like most?
- Would this solve all your problems?
- Why did you make the wrong choice?
- Are you sure?
- Would you recommend us to everyone?

Replace speculation with questions about real behavior, real decisions, and specific situations.

## 53. Post-Interview Checklist

Immediately after the interview:

- Save the recording securely
- Save notes
- Confirm participant code
- Record consent status
- Mark restricted information
- Highlight exact quotations
- Separate observations from interpretations
- Record unanswered questions
- Document contradictions
- Note possible follow-up
- Update the research tracker
- Confirm incentive delivery
- Remove unnecessary personal information
- Schedule review

## 54. Interview Quality Review

Confirm:

- The participant met eligibility requirements
- Consent was documented
- Recording permission was documented
- The interviewer avoided leading questions
- Questions focused on real behavior
- Specific examples were requested
- Exact language was preserved
- Assumptions were not presented as findings
- Sensitive information was protected
- Interpretations were labeled
- Contradictions were retained
- Evidence confidence was recorded
- Public-use permission was documented
- The participant was not pressured
- The interview remained separate from sales
