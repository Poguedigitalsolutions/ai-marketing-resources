# Campaign Planning Template

Use this template to plan coordinated marketing campaigns across content, email, social media, events, advertising, lead capture, customer follow-up, and human approval.

This resource is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> A campaign is not a pile of posts. It is a coordinated journey toward a meaningful next step.

---

## Purpose

This template helps define:

- Why the campaign exists
- Who it is designed to serve
- Where the audience is in the customer journey
- What message should remain consistent
- What offer or next step is appropriate
- Which channels and assets are required
- When each activity should occur
- Who owns each responsibility
- Where automation may assist
- Where human approval is required
- What risks must be managed
- How performance will be measured
- What should be improved after the campaign

Use it for:

- Product launches
- Service launches
- Lead-generation campaigns
- Webinar campaigns
- Event promotion
- Challenges
- Workshops
- Community launches
- Book launches
- Course launches
- Email campaigns
- Social media campaigns
- Thought-leadership campaigns
- Referral campaigns
- Re-engagement campaigns
- Customer onboarding campaigns
- Seasonal promotions
- Brand-awareness campaigns
- Local marketing campaigns
- Partnership campaigns
- Fundraising campaigns
