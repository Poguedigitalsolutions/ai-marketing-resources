# Marketing Report Template

Use this template to summarize marketing activity, performance, customer response, evidence, limitations, lessons, and recommended next actions.

This resource is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> A marketing report should explain what happened, why it may have happened, what the data cannot prove, and what the business should do next.

---

This template helps document:

- Reporting period

## 21. Return Metrics

### Return on Investment

```text
ROI = (Return - Cost) ÷ Cost × 100
```

### Return on Advertising Spend

```text
ROAS = Revenue attributed to advertising ÷ Advertising spend
```

### Customer Acquisition Cost

```text
CAC = Total acquisition cost ÷ New customers acquired
```

### Cost per Lead

```text
CPL = Campaign cost ÷ Leads generated
```
