# Editorial Calendar Template

Use this template to plan, assign, review, publish, measure, refresh, and retire content across multiple channels.

This resource is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> An editorial calendar should coordinate meaningful communication, not merely keep the publishing machine hungry.

## 13. Editorial Workflow

### Step 1: Idea Capture

Document:

- Audience need
- Business relevance
- Journey stage
- Topic
- Possible format
- Possible CTA

### Step 2: Prioritization

Evaluate:

- Audience value
- Strategic alignment
- Timeliness
- Evidence availability
- Production effort
- Campaign need
- Risk
- Existing content overlap

### Step 3: Brief

Create or approve the content brief.

### Step 4: Research

Gather approved sources and supporting evidence.

### Step 5: Draft

Create the first version.

### Step 6: Edit

Review clarity, structure, relevance, and usefulness.

### Step 7: Brand Voice Review

Confirm the content sounds recognizable and appropriate.

### Step 8: Fact and Source Review

Verify claims, dates, quotations, statistics, and links.

### Step 9: Privacy and Risk Review

Check sensitive information, consent, and confidentiality.

### Step 10: Final Approval

Record human approval.

### Step 11: Schedule

Set the publication date, time, and channel.

### Step 12: Publish

Confirm the live version works correctly.

### Step 13: Measure

Review performance and audience response.

### Step 14: Refresh or Retire

Update, repurpose, archive, or remove the content.

## 37. Approval Checklist

Before scheduling content, confirm:

- The audience is clear
- The journey stage is appropriate
- The purpose is defined
- The core message is visible
- The content brief is complete
- Sources are documented
- Facts are verified
- Claims are supported
- Brand voice is preserved
- The CTA is proportionate
- Visuals are approved
- Accessibility requirements are met
- Confidential information is protected
- Permissions are documented
- Links work
- Publication timing is correct
- A human has approved the final version
