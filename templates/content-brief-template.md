# Content Brief Template

Use this template to turn an idea, campaign goal, customer question, story, or source document into clear production instructions for human and AI-assisted content creation.

This resource is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> A strong content brief gives creativity a destination without putting it in handcuffs.

---

## Purpose

This template helps define:

- Why the content is being created
- Who it is for
- What the audience needs
- What message should remain after reading
- What sources and evidence may be used
- How the content should sound
- What format and channel requirements apply
- What action should follow
- What risks must be reviewed
- Who approves the final work

Use it for:

- Articles
- LinkedIn posts
- Email campaigns
- Landing pages
- Social media content
- Videos
- Podcasts
- Case studies
- Lead magnets
- Presentations
- Website pages
- Sales materials
- Community posts
- Training materials
- Thought leadership
- Customer education
- Marketing campaigns

## 29. Review Checklist

Before approval, confirm:

- Does the content fulfill the brief?
- Is the audience clear?
- Is the customer journey stage appropriate?
- Is the core message visible?
- Are supporting points relevant?
- Are facts accurate?
- Are sources current?
- Are claims supported?
- Are assumptions identified?
- Does the content sound like the brand?
- Is the tone appropriate?
- Are stories true and approved?
- Is the offer connection natural?
- Is the CTA proportionate?
- Is the content accessible?
- Is private information protected?
- Are legal or compliance concerns addressed?
- Does the platform format work?
- Has a human approved the final version?

A content brief should not write the content before the creator begins.
