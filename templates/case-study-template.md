# Case Study Template

Use this template to document a customer’s starting situation, decision process, implementation experience, results, evidence, limitations, and lessons without turning the customer’s story into an inflated sales claim.

This resource is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> A case study should show what happened, how it happened, and what the evidence can honestly support.

## 3. Research and Evidence Standards

The case study should:

- Use confirmed customer information
- Preserve the customer’s context
- Distinguish facts from interpretation
- Verify dates, claims, and results
- Document permission
- State important limitations
- Avoid presenting one result as typical
- Avoid implying guaranteed outcomes
- Show the customer’s role in the result
- Show the provider’s role in the result
- Acknowledge outside influences
- Include human review

The case study should not:

- Invent quotations
- Invent results
- Combine customers into a fictional customer
- Remove inconvenient context
- Exaggerate the problem
- Exaggerate the solution
- Present correlation as proof of causation
- Hide customer responsibilities
- Hide limitations
- Publish without approval
