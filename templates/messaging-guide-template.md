# Messaging Guide Template

Use this template to document how a brand explains what it does, who it serves, why it matters, what makes it different, and how its message should change across audiences, offers, channels, and customer journey stages.

This resource is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> Brand voice determines how you sound. Messaging determines what you consistently help people understand.

---

## Purpose

This template helps organize:

- Brand positioning
- Audience segments
- Customer goals and problems
- Voice-of-customer language
- Core messages
- Supporting messages
- Value propositions
- Differentiators
- Offer descriptions
- Proof and evidence
- Objection responses
- Trust-building language
- Calls to action
- Channel-specific messaging
- Claims and limitations
- Message testing

The message is clear, supportable, aligned with the brand, appropriate for the audience, and ready for human approval.

### Conditional Pass

The message is useful but requires identified revisions.

### Fail

The message is confusing, unsupported, off-brand, manipulative, unsafe, or unsuitable for the intended audience.

## 40. Approval Checklist

Before approving a message, confirm:

- Is the audience clear?
- Is the customer journey stage appropriate?
- Is the core message visible?
- Is the message relevant?
- Is the value proposition understandable?
- Are differentiators supportable?
- Are customer words used accurately?
- Are objections handled respectfully?
- Is proof documented?
- Are claims verified?
- Does the message preserve the brand voice?
- Is the CTA proportionate?
- Is the language accessible?
- Is confidential information protected?
- Are permissions documented?
- Are required disclosures included?
- Has a human approved the final version?
