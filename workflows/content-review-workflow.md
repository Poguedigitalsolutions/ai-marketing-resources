# Content Review Workflow

Use this workflow to review, correct, approve, publish, monitor, refresh, and retire human-created or AI-assisted content without sacrificing accuracy, brand voice, privacy, accessibility, source integrity, or human accountability.

This workflow is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> Polished content is not necessarily accurate, useful, safe, or ready. Review is where responsibility enters the process.

---

## Workflow Purpose

The Content Review Workflow helps organizations:

- Confirm the purpose of the content
- Identify the intended audience
- Match the content to the customer journey
- Confirm the correct channel and format
- Evaluate clarity, relevance, structure, and usefulness
- Preserve brand identity and brand voice
- Detect generic or distorted AI language
- Verify facts, sources, claims, names, dates, and calculations
- Review quotations, stories, permissions, and copyright
- Protect confidential and personal information
- Evaluate accessibility
- Review links, visuals, captions, and formatting
- Assign specialized reviewers when needed
- Record corrections
- Maintain version control
- Require human approval before execution
- Monitor published content
- Correct errors
- Refresh outdated material
- Retire content responsibly

This workflow may be used for:

- Articles
- Blog posts
- LinkedIn articles
- Social-media posts
- Email campaigns
- Newsletters
- Landing pages
- Lead magnets
- Case studies
- Marketing reports
- Advertisements
- Video scripts
- Webinar materials
- Presentations
- Sales messages
- Customer-support responses
- Community posts
- Automated communications
- Website pages
- Product descriptions
- Service descriptions
- Internal communications
- Training materials
- AI-generated drafts
- AI-assisted edits
- Repurposed content

---

# Workflow Overview

```text
Receive the content
        ↓
Confirm the objective
        ↓
Confirm the audience, journey stage, and channel
        ↓
Identify the approved version
        ↓
Review usefulness, clarity, and structure
        ↓
Review brand voice and human judgment
        ↓
Verify facts, sources, claims, and quotations
        ↓
Review stories, privacy, permissions, and copyright
        ↓
Review accessibility, visuals, links, and formatting
        ↓
Assign specialized review when required
        ↓
Record corrections
        ↓
Retest the revised version
        ↓
Human approval
        ↓
Publish, send, schedule, or activate
        ↓
Monitor, correct, refresh, or retire
```

- AI-generated citations

## Important Principle

Review is not the final cosmetic step.

It is the point where an organization confirms that the content is useful, truthful, recognizable, respectful, accessible, and safe enough to carry the brand’s name.

AI may help identify inconsistencies, compare sources, suggest edits, and prepare review notes.

A human must still decide what is true, what may be shared, what should be corrected, and whether the content is ready to create consequences in the real world.
