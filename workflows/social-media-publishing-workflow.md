# Social Media Publishing Workflow

Use this workflow to plan, create, review, approve, publish, monitor, correct, repurpose, and retire social-media content without losing brand voice, factual accuracy, privacy, accessibility, or human accountability.

This workflow is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> Social media can move quickly. Approval, accuracy, and judgment still need to arrive before the Publish button does.

---

## Workflow Purpose

The Social Media Publishing Workflow helps organizations:

- Define the purpose of each post
- Identify the intended audience
- Match the post to the customer journey
- Select the correct platform
- Capture the core message
- Connect content to an appropriate offer
- Preserve brand voice
- Adapt content to platform expectations
- Verify facts, sources, and claims
- Protect confidential information
- Document permissions
- Review images, video, audio, and links
- Improve accessibility
- Require human approval
- Schedule or publish through authorized accounts
- Monitor comments and replies
- Escalate sensitive interactions
- Correct errors
- Measure usefulness and business impact
- Repurpose approved content
- Refresh or retire outdated posts

This workflow may be used for:

- LinkedIn
- Facebook
- Instagram
- X
- TikTok
- YouTube
- YouTube Shorts
- Snapchat
- Threads

# Workflow Overview

```text
Define the objective
        ↓
Define the audience and journey stage
        ↓
Choose the platform
        ↓
Select the core message
        ↓
Gather approved context and sources
        ↓
Create the content brief
        ↓
Draft and adapt
        ↓
Review voice, facts, privacy, and permissions
        ↓
Review visual and platform requirements
        ↓
Human approval
        ↓
Schedule or publish
        ↓
Monitor and respond
        ↓
Measure, correct, repurpose, refresh, or retire
```

## Important Principle

Social media rewards speed, but a fast mistake can travel farther than a careful correction.

AI may help draft, adapt, organize, and repurpose the message.

A human must still decide what deserves to be published, who should receive it, and how the organization will respond when people answer back.
