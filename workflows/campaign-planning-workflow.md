# Campaign Planning Workflow

Use this workflow to plan, review, approve, launch, monitor, optimize, correct, refresh, and retire marketing campaigns without sacrificing strategic clarity, customer relevance, brand voice, factual accuracy, privacy, accessibility, or human accountability.

This workflow is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> A campaign is not a pile of content with matching colors. It is a coordinated journey designed to help the right audience take the right next step.

---

## Workflow Purpose

The Campaign Planning Workflow helps organizations:

- Define the campaign objective
- Identify the intended audience
- Clarify the customer problem
- Define the desired customer outcome
- Select the correct customer journey stage
- Confirm the offer
- Establish positioning
- Develop campaign messaging
- Select appropriate channels
- Plan content themes
- Inventory required assets
- Assign ownership
- Establish deadlines
- Approve budgets
- Define lead capture
- Plan follow-up
- Create sales handoffs
- Configure automation
- Verify facts and claims
- Protect privacy
- Confirm permissions
- Review accessibility
- Identify dependencies
- Define approval gates
- Test the campaign
- Require human approval
- Launch responsibly
- Monitor performance
- Evaluate lead quality
- Optimize without damaging trust
- Correct failures
- Refresh campaign materials
- Retire outdated campaigns

This workflow may be used for:

- Product launches
- Service launches
- Lead-generation campaigns
- Brand-awareness campaigns
- Content campaigns
- Email campaigns
- Social-media campaigns
- Webinar campaigns
- Event campaigns
- Workshop campaigns
- Challenge campaigns
- Community launches
- Newsletter growth
- Customer-retention campaigns
- Re-engagement campaigns
- Referral campaigns
- Partnership campaigns
- Affiliate campaigns
- Paid advertising campaigns
- Search campaigns
- Outreach campaigns
- Seasonal campaigns
- Promotional campaigns
- Educational campaigns
- Recruitment campaigns
- Internal campaigns
- Multi-channel campaigns

# Workflow Overview

```text
Define the campaign objective
        ↓
Define the audience
        ↓
Identify the customer journey stage
        ↓
Confirm the offer
        ↓
Develop positioning and messaging
        ↓
Select channels
        ↓
Plan content themes and assets
        ↓
Build timeline, budget, and responsibilities
        ↓
Configure lead capture, follow-up, sales handoffs, and automation
        ↓
Review claims, privacy, permissions, accessibility, and risk
        ↓
Test the complete campaign
        ↓
Human approval
        ↓
Launch
        ↓
Monitor performance, lead quality, and customer response
        ↓
Optimize, correct, refresh, pause, or retire
```

## Important Principle

A campaign should not begin with a calendar full of posts.

It should begin with a real customer, a clear problem, a useful offer, and a deliberate path from attention to action.

AI may help research, organize, draft, test, schedule, route, and report.

A human must still decide what the campaign should promise, who it should reach, what it may ask of them, and whether the entire system deserves to carry the brand’s name.
