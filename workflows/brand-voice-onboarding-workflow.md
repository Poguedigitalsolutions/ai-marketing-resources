# Brand Voice Onboarding Workflow

Use this workflow to discover, document, test, approve, operationalize, govern, refresh, and eventually offboard a brand voice without reducing the organization to a few adjectives or allowing AI-generated assumptions to become brand truth.

This workflow is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> A brand voice is not invented during onboarding. It is discovered, organized, tested, and approved.

---

## Workflow Purpose

The Brand Voice Onboarding Workflow helps organizations:

- Define the business and brand accurately
- Clarify mission, purpose, values, vision, and positioning
- Identify audiences and customer journey stages
- Document offers, claims, boundaries, and responsibilities
- Capture founder and subject-matter expertise
- Collect approved examples of real communication
- Organize stories, analogies, frameworks, and signature language
- Define voice characteristics and tone ranges
- Identify phrases, habits, and behaviors to avoid
- Separate approved knowledge from assumptions
- Remove outdated or contradictory information
- Establish a source-of-truth library
- Build initial AI instructions
- Create review criteria
- Test the voice across channels and situations
- Protect privacy, permissions, and confidential information
- Require human approval before operational use
- Train team members
- Establish governance and version control
- Monitor brand drift
- Refresh the voice over time
- Offboard access, tools, contractors, and obsolete materials safely

This workflow may be used for:

- Founder-led businesses
- Solopreneurs
- Coaches
- Consultants
- Service businesses
- Agencies
- Marketing teams
- Sales teams
- Customer-support teams
- Community teams
- Nonprofits
- Educational organizations
- Veteran-owned businesses
- Government contractors
- Personal brands
- Company brands
- Sub-brands
- Product brands
- Executive communication
- AI assistants
- Custom GPTs
- Knowledge bases
- Content systems
- Marketing automations

# Workflow Overview

```text
Define the business
        ↓
Define the audience and offers
        ↓
Collect source material
        ↓
Interview the founder and key experts
        ↓
Separate approved knowledge from assumptions
        ↓
Capture voice, tone, rhythm, stories, and judgment
        ↓
Define boundaries and prohibited behavior
        ↓
Organize the knowledge library
        ↓
Build AI instructions and examples
        ↓
Test across channels and situations
        ↓
Review and correct
        ↓
Human approval
        ↓
Operational rollout
        ↓
Monitor, govern, refresh, or offboard
```

## Important Principle

A brand voice is more than tone, vocabulary, or style.

It carries identity, experience, values, judgment, stories, boundaries, and responsibility.

AI may help organize and apply those elements.

A human must decide which sources are true, which stories may be told, which promises may be made, and whether the final communication still belongs to the brand.
