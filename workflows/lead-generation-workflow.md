# Lead Generation Workflow

Use this workflow to plan, create, review, approve, launch, monitor, improve, pause, and retire lead-generation systems without sacrificing audience fit, consent, privacy, brand voice, factual accuracy, customer trust, or human accountability.

This workflow is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> A lead is not merely a name in a database. It is a person who has taken a step toward solving a problem.

---

## Workflow Purpose

The Lead Generation Workflow helps organizations:

- Define the business objective
- Identify the intended audience
- Clarify the offer
- Establish lead qualification criteria
- Map the customer journey
- Select appropriate acquisition channels
- Create lead magnets and conversion assets
- Build landing pages and forms
- Capture consent
- Minimize personal-data collection
- Verify claims, pricing, deadlines, and links
- Route leads into the correct CRM stage
- Assign ownership
- Develop responsible follow-up
- Create sales handoffs
- Establish suppression and exit rules
- Require human approval
- Measure lead quality
- Measure conversion and cost
- Review attribution
- Monitor complaints and failures
- Correct broken workflows
- Refresh offers
- Retire outdated campaigns

This workflow may be used for:

- Lead magnets
- Downloadable guides
- Assessments
- Checklists
- Templates
- Webinars
- Workshops
- Challenges
- Events
- Consultations
- Demonstrations
- Discovery calls
- Waitlists
- Newsletter growth
- Community invitations
- Referral programs
- Partner campaigns
- Social-media campaigns
- Content campaigns
- Search campaigns
- Paid advertising
- Outbound outreach
- Direct messages
- Email campaigns
- Landing pages
- Forms
- Chatbots
- CRM workflows
- Marketing automation
