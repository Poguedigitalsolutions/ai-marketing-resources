# Article Creation Workflow

Use this workflow to turn an idea, observation, story, question, framework, customer problem, or research topic into a credible, brand-aligned article that receives human review before publication.

This workflow is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> Open with something worth noticing. Use AI to organize the work. Close with something worth remembering.

---

## Workflow Purpose

The Article Creation Workflow helps writers and organizations:

- Define the purpose of an article
- Identify the intended audience
- Match the article to the customer journey
- Capture the core idea or argument
- Gather approved source material
- Verify facts and claims
- Develop a useful structure
- Use AI for research, organization, drafting, and editing
- Preserve founder knowledge and brand voice
- Add stories and practical examples
- Protect confidential information
- Review permissions and copyright
- Improve accessibility
- Obtain human approval
- Publish through the correct channel
- Repurpose the approved article
- Monitor performance
- Refresh or retire outdated content

The workflow may be used for:

- LinkedIn articles
- Writing.io articles
- Blog posts
- Newsletters
- Thought-leadership articles
- Educational articles
- Founder essays
- Customer-problem articles
- Research summaries
- Framework explanations
- Case-study articles
- Industry commentary
- Opinion pieces
- How-to articles
- Comparison articles
- List articles
- Story-based articles

---

## 34. Draft One Section at a Time

Recommended sequence:

1. Confirm the objective.
2. Confirm the sources.
3. Confirm the claim register.
4. Approve the outline.
5. Draft the opening.
6. Draft section one.
7. Verify section one.
8. Draft section two.
9. Verify section two.
10. Continue until the middle is complete.
11. Add human interpretation.
12. Draft the close.
13. Complete final review.

This reduces the risk of polished errors hiding inside a long article.

## 64. Repurposing Rules

Each derivative should:

- Preserve the article’s core truth
- Preserve verified claims
- Preserve source integrity
- Preserve brand voice
- Fit the platform
- Fit the journey stage
- Use an appropriate CTA
- Receive human review

Avoid:

- Identical copies everywhere
- New facts added without verification
- Stronger claims in shorter formats
- Removed qualifications
- Misleading excerpts
- Repetitive openings
- Overusing the same story
- Turning every derivative into a sales pitch
