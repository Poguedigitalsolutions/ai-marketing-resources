# Email Development Workflow

Use this workflow to plan, write, review, test, approve, send, monitor, correct, refresh, and retire brand-aligned email communication without sacrificing accuracy, consent, privacy, accessibility, or human accountability.

This workflow is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> An email should earn its place in the inbox before it asks for attention, trust, or action.

---

## Workflow Purpose

The Email Development Workflow helps organizations:

- Define the purpose of an email
- Identify the intended audience
- Match communication to the customer journey
- Select the correct email type
- Clarify the offer and call to action
- Develop subject lines and preview text
- Write useful openings, body content, and conclusions
- Preserve brand voice
- Use AI for research, organization, drafting, and editing
- Verify facts, claims, prices, dates, and links
- Review personalization
- Protect confidential information
- Confirm consent and eligibility
- Improve accessibility
- Test formatting and delivery
- Require human approval
- Send or activate automation responsibly
- Monitor replies, engagement, complaints, and conversions
- Correct errors
- Refresh or retire outdated email assets

This workflow may be used for:

- Newsletters
- Welcome emails
- Lead-nurture emails
- Educational sequences
- Sales emails
- Promotional campaigns
- Event invitations
- Registration confirmations
- Reminder emails
- Follow-up emails
- Customer onboarding
- Product announcements
- Service announcements
- Re-engagement campaigns
- Renewal communication
- Customer-support follow-up
- Survey invitations
- Referral requests
- Community invitations
- Abandoned-form follow-up
- Automated lifecycle communication
- Transactional email
- Internal communication

---

# Workflow Overview

```text
Define the objective
        ↓
Define the audience and journey stage
        ↓
Select the email type
        ↓
Confirm consent and eligibility
        ↓
Define the offer, message, and CTA
        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
Develop subject line and preview text
        ↓
Draft the opening, body, and close
        ↓
Review brand voice, facts, privacy, and accessibility
        ↓
Test links, personalization, formatting, and delivery
        ↓
Human approval
        ↓
Send or activate
        ↓
Monitor replies, results, and risks
        ↓
Correct, improve, refresh, or retire
```

## Important Principle

An email is not successful merely because it was delivered, opened, or clicked.

It should reach the right person, for the right reason, with a useful message, a clear next step, and a respectful path to reply or leave.

AI may help organize, draft, test, and adapt the communication.

A human must still decide who should receive it, what promises may be made, and whether the message deserves to enter someone’s inbox.
