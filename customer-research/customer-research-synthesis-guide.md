# Customer Research Synthesis Guide

Use this guide to prepare, classify, compare, synthesize, review, approve, apply, refresh, and retire customer research findings without stripping away context, uncertainty, contradictions, minority viewpoints, or the human experiences behind the evidence.

This guide is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> Research synthesis is not the process of making every customer sound as though they agree. It is the process of building an honest map of where the evidence converges, where it divides, what remains unknown, and which decisions the evidence can responsibly support.

---

## Guide Purpose

The Customer Research Synthesis Guide helps organizations:

- Prepare research evidence for analysis
- Preserve original source material
- Protect consent, privacy, and permissions
- Separate source records from working notes
- Develop a coding system
- Code evidence without losing context
- Group related evidence
- Identify patterns
- Develop themes
- Compare audience segments
- Compare customer journey stages
- Distinguish frequency from severity
- Distinguish confidence from importance
- Preserve contradictions
- Preserve exceptions
- Preserve minority viewpoints
- Separate facts from observations
- Separate observations from interpretations
- Label assumptions
- Record unknowns
- Develop research findings
- Build evidence-backed recommendations
- Connect findings to business decisions
- Apply findings to positioning, offers, messaging, content, sales, customer experience, and AI systems
- Use AI responsibly for clustering and summarization
- Require human verification
- Maintain version control
- Refresh outdated synthesis
- Retire unsupported findings

This guide may be used with:

- Customer interviews
- Prospect interviews
- Lost-deal interviews
- Churn interviews
- Surveys
- Sales conversations
- Support conversations
- Reviews
- Testimonials

- Search-language research

- Event feedback
- Partner feedback
- CRM notes
- Usability studies
- Customer journey research
- Voice of Customer research
- Customer evidence libraries
- AI knowledge systems

```text

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
Develop a coding framework
        ↓
Code evidence
        ↓

        ↓
Identify patterns
        ↓
Develop themes
        ↓
Review frequency, severity, confidence, and importance
        ↓
Preserve contradictions and minority viewpoints
        ↓

        ↓
Develop findings
        ↓
Develop recommendations
        ↓
Human verification
        ↓
Approve applications
        ↓
Refresh, archive, or retire
```

# Phase Eight: Develop Themes

## 36. Theme Definition

A theme is a broader interpretation that connects several patterns and explains why they matter.

Example:

```text
Theme:
Customers do not primarily struggle with content volume. They struggle with preserving identity while producing content consistently.
```

This theme may connect patterns involving:

- Rewriting AI output
- Inconsistent tone
- Founder approval bottlenecks
- Fear of sounding generic
- Difficulty delegating content

# Phase Nine: Evaluate Frequency

Frequency describes how often evidence appeared within the available research set.

# Phase Ten: Evaluate Severity

## 43. Severity Definition

Severity describes the potential impact of an issue.

### Low

Minor inconvenience with limited consequence.

### Moderate

Meaningful friction, delay, confusion, or cost.

### High

Significant financial, operational, customer, emotional, or reputational consequence.

### Critical

Potential safety, legal, privacy, accessibility, severe financial, or serious human harm.

## Important Principle

Research synthesis should not sand every rough edge from the evidence until the customer story fits neatly inside a presentation slide.

The disagreements matter.

The exceptions matter.

The low-frequency concerns matter.

The unknowns matter.

AI may help organize, code, cluster, compare, retrieve, summarize, and format approved evidence.

A human must still decide what the evidence supports, what remains uncertain, what deserves action, and whether the final recommendation respects the people whose experiences made the research possible.
