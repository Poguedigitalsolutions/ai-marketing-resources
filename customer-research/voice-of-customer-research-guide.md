# Voice of Customer Research Guide

Use this guide to collect, organize, verify, interpret, and apply real customer language across messaging, offers, content, sales, customer experience, and AI-assisted systems.

This guide is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> Voice of Customer research is not the art of making customers sound more persuasive. It is the discipline of listening closely enough to understand how they describe their problems, priorities, fears, decisions, and desired outcomes in their own words.

---

The Voice of Customer Research Guide helps organizations:

- Define the research decision
- Identify the correct audience
- Select appropriate research methods
- Recruit participants responsibly

- Review surveys
- Analyze sales and support conversations
- Collect direct customer language
- Separate evidence from assumptions
- Identify recurring themes

- Connect findings to the customer journey
- Improve positioning

- Improve sales conversations

- Require human review

# Phase Seven: Develop Interview Questions

## 33. Question Principles

Questions should be:

- Neutral
- Open-ended
- Specific
- Easy to understand
- Focused on real experiences
- Free from hidden sales pitches
- Free from assumed answers
- Appropriate to the journey stage
- Respectful of privacy
- Flexible enough for follow-up

---

## 34. Avoid Leading Questions

Avoid:

```text
How much did our excellent service help you?
```

Use:

```text
What changed after you began using the service?
```

Avoid:

```text
Wouldn’t you agree that consistency is your biggest challenge?
```

Use:

```text
What makes consistency difficult in your current process?
```

Avoid:

```text
How likely are you to buy this innovative solution?
```

Use:

```text
How would you evaluate whether this solution is worth considering?
```

---

## 35. Opening Questions

- What are you responsible for?

## 52. Interview Opening

Use:

```text
Thank you for taking the time to speak with me.

I am here to understand your experience, not to sell you anything or evaluate your answers. There are no correct responses. I may ask follow-up questions so I can understand the situation in your own words.

You may skip any question or stop at any time.
```

## 53. Interviewer Conduct

The interviewer should:

- Listen more than speak
- Avoid completing the participant’s sentences
- Ask for examples
- Ask what happened next
- Clarify unfamiliar language
- Preserve pauses
- Avoid defending the business
- Avoid correcting the participant
- Avoid turning the interview into support or sales
- Record interpretation separately from quotations
- Respect emotional boundaries
- Stop when appropriate

---

- What did you mean by that word?
- How did you decide?
- What made you feel that way?
- Compared with what?
- How often did that happen?
- Who else was involved?
- What would success have looked like?

- Teaching

- Promising changes

- Whether the participant was describing themselves
- Whether the statement concerned someone else
- Whether the language was literal, emotional, or hypothetical
- Whether the participant later contradicted it

---

## 69. Quotation Accuracy

Confirm:

- [ ] The quotation is exact.
- [ ] The speaker is correct.
- [ ] Context is preserved.
- [ ] Paraphrases are labeled.
- [ ] Grammar is not polished without disclosure.
- [ ] Meaning is not changed.
- [ ] Permission status is recorded.
- [ ] AI did not generate or complete the quotation.

---

## 70. Avoid Manufactured Voice of Customer

Do not:

- Combine multiple customers into one quotation
- Rewrite a customer quote to make it more persuasive
- Invent a quote based on a theme
- Turn internal language into customer language
- Present AI-generated phrases as research
- Remove qualifications that change meaning
- Use an unusual phrase as if it were common
- Treat a founder’s belief as customer evidence

## Important Principle

Customers should not be reduced to data points, personas, conversion rates, or convenient quotations.

Voice of Customer research is valuable because it restores texture to the customer journey. It reveals the words people use, the decisions they face, the fears they carry, the alternatives they consider, and the outcomes they are trying to reach.

AI may help organize, code, compare, summarize, retrieve, and apply customer evidence.

A human must still decide what the evidence supports, what remains uncertain, what may be shared, and whether the final message respects the people whose voices made it possible.
