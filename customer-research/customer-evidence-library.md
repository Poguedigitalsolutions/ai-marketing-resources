# Customer Evidence Library

Use this resource to collect, organize, classify, verify, govern, retrieve, apply, refresh, and retire customer evidence without losing the original context, permissions, segment distinctions, uncertainty, or human meaning behind the information.

This resource is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> Customer evidence should remain connected to the person, situation, source, context, and limitations that gave it meaning. A quotation removed from its context can become a costume worn by a conclusion the customer never intended.

---

## Resource Purpose

The Customer Evidence Library helps organizations:

- Preserve exact customer quotations
- Record the original context
- Identify audience segments
- Identify customer journey stages
- Organize problems, triggers, objections, alternatives, and desired outcomes
- Document source records
- Track consent and permissions
- Protect privacy
- Separate facts from memories
- Separate observations from interpretations
- Label assumptions
- Preserve contradictory evidence
- Assign confidence levels
- Identify recurring themes
- Preserve minority viewpoints
- Approve specific uses
- Support messaging
- Support offers
- Support content
- Support sales
- Support customer experience
- Ground AI-assisted systems
- Control access
- Assign review dates
- Refresh outdated evidence
- Retire unsupported records
- Maintain human accountability

This library may contain evidence from:

- Customer interviews
- Prospect interviews
- Lost-deal interviews
- Churn interviews
- Surveys
- Sales calls
- Support conversations
- Reviews

---

## 27. Manufactured Voice of Customer Prohibition

Do not:

- Rewrite a quotation to make it more persuasive
- Combine phrases from several participants
- Remove qualifications that change meaning
- Convert a theme into a quotation
- Present internal language as customer language
- Present AI-generated language as customer evidence
- Treat a memorable phrase as a common phrase without evidence
- Publish restricted quotations

## Important Principle

Customer evidence is not raw material to be mined until it says what the business wants.

It is a record of real people describing real situations from particular points in their journeys. The value comes from preserving that texture, including uncertainty, contradiction, permission, and context.

AI may help organize, classify, compare, retrieve, summarize, and apply approved evidence.

A human must still decide whether the evidence is trustworthy, what it supports, what may be shared, and whether the final use respects the people behind the records.
