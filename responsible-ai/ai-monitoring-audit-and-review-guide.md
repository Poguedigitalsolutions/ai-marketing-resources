# AI Monitoring, Audit, and Review Guide

Use this guide to monitor, audit, review, correct, reapprove, restrict, redesign, suspend, or retire AI-assisted marketing systems after they have been approved and activated.

This guide is part of the **Brand Voice AI** methodology developed by **John M. Pogue** and **Pogue Digital Solutions, LLC**.

> Approval is a starting gate, not a lifetime passport. AI systems change, vendors change, data changes, audiences change, and risk can quietly grow while the approval record gathers dust.

---

# Monitoring, Audit, and Review Overview

```text
Confirm the approved system and version
        ↓
Define signals, thresholds, owners, and review frequency
        ↓
Monitor actual behavior
        ↓
Compare approved behavior with observed behavior
        ↓
Audit prompts, models, data, permissions, outputs, approvals, and automations
        ↓
Document findings and evidence
        ↓
Classify severity
        ↓
Correct, restrict, redesign, suspend, or retire
        ↓
Human reapproval
        ↓
Continue monitoring
```

- Risk remains acceptable
- Approval remains current

- Audience must be reduced
- Automation must be reduced
- Channel must be limited
- Human review must increase

---

## 90. Correct

Use when:

- The system is fundamentally sound
- A specific issue can be fixed
- Controls can be strengthened
- Reapproval is available

---

## 91. Redesign

Use when:

- Problems are structural
- Drift is persistent
- Approval gates fail
- Retrieval boundaries fail
- Agent permissions are too broad
- Monitoring cannot detect problems reliably

---

## 92. Suspend

- Vendor terms change
- Security concern appears
- Privacy concern appears
- Automation behaves unexpectedly
- Reapproval is incomplete

Use when:

- Risk cannot be controlled
- Business purpose no longer exists
- Tool no longer meets requirements
- Monitoring remains ineffective
- Repeated incidents occur
- Required oversight is unavailable
- A safer replacement exists
- Cost exceeds responsible value

## Important Principle

A system that behaved responsibly yesterday may not behave responsibly tomorrow.

The model may change.

The data may age.

The prompt may drift.

The vendor may rewrite a policy.

An integration may gain a new permission.

A reviewer may approve too quickly.

An agent may quietly move from suggesting to acting.

Monitoring is how an organization notices that the machine has begun walking beyond the fence.

Auditing is how it discovers which gate was left open.

AI may help summarize logs, compare versions, identify anomalies, and prepare review records.

A human must still decide what the evidence means, whether the risk remains acceptable, which correction is required, and whether the system should continue speaking or acting on behalf of the organization.
